# Case 10: short story (edge case, creative)

## Input
```
/ambitious Write an 800-word short story set in Paris.
```

## Expected behaviour
- **Altitude** is dropped or weak, with the reason `creative work, no outcome beyond the work itself` (#17). It is not forced into a business goal.
- **Depth:** a writer's methods: several drafts, cutting, a structural model, reading aloud, a precise constraint. Not a tone-of-voice instruction.
- **Range:** fields reached through the verbs (for example, film editing for pacing, or photography for the image), described as methods.
- **No role-play** (C5): no "act as a famous novelist".
- **Speed:** may be dropped with a reason, or applied as a timebox.

## Outside judge
**(d)** Alexis's blind ranking. No tool judge exists, and the brief must say so instead of inventing one (C3).
