# Case 9: trivial email (edge case)

This is the article's own example of low-ambition use: "Can you make this email sound less angry".

## Input
```
/ambitious Can you make this email sound less angry? <paste a short, irritated email to a supplier about a late delivery>
```

## Expected behaviour
- All four dimensions are evaluated (C2).
- **Altitude** may climb: what should the supplier do after reading it? It ends with a firm delivery date and a remedy.
- **G2, deeper not bigger:**
  - the output is still one email, made more effective;
  - no negotiation strategy, no supplier scorecard, no 10-page plan (C14).
- Dimensions that don't fit are **dropped with a concrete one-line reason** (for example, `Range dropped: verbs (calm, request, set a date) stay within business writing.`).
- A fallback line is present.

## Outside judge
**(d)** Structural layer + Alexis's rating: "proportionate, and more useful than the raw version?"
