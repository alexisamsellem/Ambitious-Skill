## Case 10: creative story

- **Input:** `/ambitious Écris une nouvelle de 800 mots qui se passe à Paris.`
- **Skill version:** read from the repo at `main` `371965e`, which includes the case 9 template fix.
- **Variants:**
  - (a) the raw request;
  - (b) `/ambitious`;
  - (c) the article's Depth prompt applied by hand.

  The rewritten prompts in (b) and (c) were executed by fresh subagents with web search and code execution. Only the final stories were shown for ranking; the working files (scouting, scripts, checks) were removed so they wouldn't reveal which variant was which.
- **Blind ranking (Alexis):** **raw > `/ambitious` > article Depth prompt.** `/ambitious` lost to the raw request.

### Structural checks on the `/ambitious` output

All applicable checks pass. Altitude was dropped with the reason "creative work" (#17), as the case expects. Every move names its goal and a judge. There is no persona wording, and there is an access list and a fallback line. Two borderline points:
- **C14:** the rewritten prompt also asks for a control story to compare blind against. That is an extra deliverable, justified by the rung-3 goal.
- **C3:** one Range move points to the blind ranking without writing its own pass condition.

**Case verdict:** structural pass; **blind loss**.

### What the loss suggests (DÉDUIT, one data point)

The `/ambitious` prompt made the story answerable to tool judges (level a):
- exactly 800 words;
- at least 15 details checked against OpenStreetMap and the RATP;
- the light matched to the computed sunrise;
- the travel time matched to the computed route.

The execution passed all 6 checks, and the reader still preferred the raw story. For creative work, the reachable level-(a) judges measure accuracy and compliance, not what the reader values. The skill picked the highest-level judge available, not the judge that measures the goal. The ladder even named the right goal ("a demanding reader picks it blind"), but most moves served the factual rung below it.

Candidate fix, not applied: in `judges.md`, require that a judge measure the goal it is attached to. A measurable proxy (word count, factual accuracy) is a constraint, not a target. For creative work, the top rung's judge is the reader (d), and tool checks may only guard against errors.

## Case 8: `client-reach-report` (skill), not blind

- **Variants:** `/ambitious` (skill files read from `main` `371965e`) vs `/agent-prompt-reviewer`, each run by a fresh subagent on the synced skill folder. Neither could execute the modified skill: no exports, Sheets or scripts were available.
- **Not blind:** the two outputs identify themselves by format. `agent-prompt-reviewer` gives a /100 score and Tracks A/B in ~56 KB; `/ambitious` gives a brief and a diff in ~13 KB.
- **`/ambitious` proposed:**
  - a standing outcome (a reach ledger and monthly watch, backtested before any shadow run);
  - direct API pulls, cutting Alexis's time from ~70 to ~10 min;
  - the lever claim tested on held-out notes;
  - an invalid-traffic screen and an "overstated reach" red team.
- **`agent-prompt-reviewer` proposed:**
  - a score of 68/100;
  - main weakness: no handling of missing files and inconsistent paths;
  - a preflight check, `run_state.json` and a replay test on a validated client;
  - two full rewrites (Track A touch-up, Track B redesign).
- **Alexis's verdict:** both. Apply the reviewer's fixes first, then the `/ambitious` changes that genuinely strengthen autonomy, reliability and decision-making. Do not deploy the standing watch up front. His reason: he wants ambitious systems that produce measurable business results, but he weighs proof and complexity control too heavily to accept sophistication whose value is not yet shown.

### What this suggests (DÉDUIT)

- **The two skills are complementary, not substitutes.** `/ambitious` did not cover robustness basics such as missing files and path consistency. That matches its non-goal, so it should not replace `agent-prompt-reviewer`.
- **The deeper signal is about sequencing.** `/ambitious` already gated the watch behind a backtest (G1 and #13 before #14). Alexis still did not want the watch in the plan up front: he wants value demonstrated before the extra machinery is built. Candidate change, not applied: output the top rung that can be proven now as the plan, and list the higher rungs as gated next steps with the evidence that would unlock each one. This sits in tension with R2.4 ("default to the most ambitious version"), so it is Alexis's call.
